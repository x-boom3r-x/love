My dear Imane,

I don't know what the future has written for us, but I know what I would like to build with you.

I want us to learn how to listen to each other, not only when everything is beautiful, but especially when things become difficult. I want your voice to have a place beside mine, and your feelings to be treated with the care they deserve.

I want us to disagree without hurting each other, to speak honestly without fear, and to understand that being right is never more important than treating each other with respect.

I cannot promise that we will never have difficult days. I cannot promise to always find the perfect words. But I can promise to keep learning, to be more attentive, and to never take what we have for granted.

I hope we create a life filled with little moments: spontaneous laughter, peaceful evenings, shared dreams, silly conversations, and the comfort of knowing that we are on the same side.

I don't want us to simply return to what we were. I want us to become something better than before. Something honest. Something peaceful. Something we can both be proud of.

And if life gives us the chance, I hope one day we will look back at these pages and smile, knowing that this was the moment we chose to write a different story.

With all my heart,
Yours, always in hope.
